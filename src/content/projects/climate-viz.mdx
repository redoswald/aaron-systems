I built Climate Viz way, way back, in the second COVID spring, while living in New York City. I largely had a feeling of wanting to get out from the grayness and the coldness, and wanted to find what a perfect climate was.

## How it works

Roughly, I grabbed climate data for all of the world's cities, plotted it out in Tableau, and then calculated a similarity score based on sunlight and temperature over the course of 12 months. It's just the Pythagorean distance between two cities: the difference in temperature, squared, plus the difference in sunlight, squared, and take the square root of that. That gets you your distance.

## What I learned

It was very useful. I feel like I take for granted how much I now know about where in the world has similar and different climates. Now there are lots of maps you can find online that say things like: the climate of D.C. is most similar to the climate of the Po River Valley in Italy. Everybody knows that the West Coast has the best climate in the United States, but they might not know that there are random pockets in Colombia or China that also have incredibly beautiful climates.

*Spider-Man: Brand New Day* was an okay movie. It doesn't necessarily have all the same Marvel feeling that I expected, and it still feels very Sony-y at times, particularly during some of the swinging scenes. It definitely seems to almost be a callback to the original *Spider-Man 2*.

It was at times a tearjerker, but it didn't feel totally unearned.

It did make me interested in going back and actually reviewing all the Marvel movies I've been ignoring since *Endgame*. Maybe that can be a holiday project.

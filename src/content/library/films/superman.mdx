The James Gunn Superman movie was good. It felt like the first superhero film in a long time that didn’t make me feel guilty for watching one. The reason is simple: it avoided getting too “cinematic-universe” heavy. Sure, it hinted that it exists within a broader universe, but it didn’t spend its runtime trying to hype me up for sequels or spin-offs. It was just a fun watch.

The visuals still had that overly glossy, video-game feel that so many blockbusters suffer from—most obvious in a strange “Proton River” sequence that seemed squarely aimed at kids used to gaming aesthetics. But the film made up for it with plenty of funny characters, and the chemistry between Lois Lane and Clark Kent was refreshing. It actually felt like real sexual tension, which made me think maybe the era of sexless blockbusters is finally ending and romance has returned.

And Lex Luthor? Great. This was the first time in a while I felt like Luthor was genuinely compelling again, a character you wanted to watch rather than just a stock villain.
